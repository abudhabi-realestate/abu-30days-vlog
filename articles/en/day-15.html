Day 15 | Don't Be Fooled by "Island": Same-Island Prices Can Differ 2.5x, and Handover Timing Is a Hidden Risk

Abu Dhabi Real Estate 30 Days · Day 15 · Based on ADREC 2025 public data

Over the past year, most clients who came to me about Abu Dhabi were first drawn in by one word: "Island" — Yas, Saadiyat, Hidariyat. Sounds premium, right?

Days 6—9 broke down each island's positioning, amenities and pricing — but that was island vs island. Day 15 pours cold water on inside the island: the word "Island" itself is being over-marketed.

01 | How big is an island, really?

The smallest of the three, Yas Island, is about 25 km² — roughly 3,500 standard football pitches. That's not a small romantic islet you can see across in one glance — it's a densely built city district: buildings, highways, interchanges everywhere. From many units, you simply can't see the sea.

Unless your unit directly faces the water, living on the island vs living inland offers no meaningful difference in view — but the "island" label alone can still push up the asking price. Before buying, ask: what's actually outside my window — the sea, or the neighboring building's wall?

02 | Same island, prices can differ 2.5x — the Saadiyat case

Saadiyat's headline average is often quoted around ~AED 40,000/m², which sounds uniformly premium.

But walk the island and you'll find Saadiyat Lagoons — located in the island's central-southeast area — averaging only ~AED 15,000/m², less than 40% of the headline figure. Most clients don't believe it the first time they hear it.

Saadiyat overall (incl. beachfront prime)Saadiyat Lagoons (secondary location)
Avg price~AED 40,000/m²~AED 15,000/m²
LocationCultural district / beachfrontCentral-southeast of the island
Premium sourceSea view, proximity to cultural landmarks, branded developersWell-planned community, non-prime position

Different locations within the same island can differ by more than 2x. "The island average" is a rough headline number — what actually sets your price is the specific location within the island, not the island's name.

03 | Always check handover year on resale units — waterproofing is a hidden risk

Rainfall events in the UAE have clearly increased in recent years (April 2024 saw historic extreme rainfall). Projects delivered earlier were often designed for a dry climate, and waterproofing wasn't a design priority at the time.

Early-handover projectsRecently delivered / new off-plan
Climate assumptionDry year-round, waterproofing not a focusExtreme rain more frequent, higher waterproofing standards
RiskHigher chance of roof / facade / basement leaksNewer design codes, relatively lower risk
CostRepair costs can be significant once leaks appear—

This is part of why more local buyers are now choosing new off-plan — not just for novelty, but for higher construction standards and updated design codes.

When viewing a ready unit, don't just ask price and size — ask the handover year and whether there's any leak/repair history. Many buyers only discover this cost a year or two after moving in.

04 | What actually matters: three things, not the island name

My standing advice to clients: stop fixating on "which island" or "how much cheaper than the neighborhood."

What genuinely matters:

DimensionWhy it matters
Community amenitiesDrives daily convenience and long-term rental appeal — more concrete than the island's name
Product quality (incl. handover year / leak history)Directly affects future maintenance cost and living experience
The unit's own viewThe "island premium" only pays off if the unit genuinely faces the sea / a real view

These three drive your living experience and value retention — the word "Island" by itself does not.

05 | Common myths vs the right questions

Common mythAsk this instead
"It's on XX Island, so it'll hold value"What does this specific unit actually look out onto?
"Cheaper than other projects on the island — good deal"Is it cheap because of weak location, or genuinely weaker product?
"Ready property feels safer than off-plan"What year was it handed over? Any leak/repair history?
"Island premium is just how it is"Which part of the island premium am I actually getting — the view, the amenities, or just the name?

Summary

Days 6—9 were about choosing between islands. Day 15 is about seeing clearly within one.

Five takeaways:

  1. Yas Island is ~25 km² — about 3,500 football pitches. Density is high; without a direct sea-facing unit, you likely won't see the sea.
  2. Within the same Saadiyat Island, Lagoons can price at under 40% of the beachfront headline average — over 2x spread inside one island.
  3. UAE rainfall events are increasing; earlier-handover ready units may have weaker waterproofing, with real future repair costs.
  4. More local buyers are shifting to new off-plan for better construction standards and updated design — not just "newness."
  5. What really drives living experience and value retention: community amenities, product quality, and the unit's own view — not the word "Island."
